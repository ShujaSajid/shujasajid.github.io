I am currently a PhD Scholar in the Department of Computer Science at Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi. I also work as Guest Faculty at the Centre for Interdisciplinary Research in Basic Sciences, where I teach Computer Science to BSc programmes.

My research interests are in cybersecurity, knowledge representation, ontology, and ergonomics — with a current focus on tactical risk scoring and decision-process frameworks for healthcare vulnerabilities.
